Custom Vitals
This form captures essential vital signs measurements excluding:
- Weight in kg
- Height & length (measured annually)
- Temperature in F
- O2 flow rate
- Inhaled O2 concentration
- Head & waist circumference
- BMI

Included fields:
- Systolic BP (mmHg)
- Diastolic BP (mmHg)
- Pulse (bpm)
- Respiration (per min)
- Oxygen Saturation (%)
- Mean Arterial Pressure (MAP) (20-150 mmHg)
- Notes

This form is designed for regular vital signs monitoring without the excluded measurements.
